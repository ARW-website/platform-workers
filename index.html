VOICES OF THE PLATFORM ECONOMY

One system.
Different realities.

Therefore, platform workers insist on concluding employment contracts, which would, according to them, mitigate present issues. They claim that establishing labour relations with platforms would also hinder arbitrary tariff changes or unjustified account suspensions, as they are highly dissatisfied with the current civil relations model, which allows for such unilateral decisions by platforms. Nonetheless, employment contracts would make platform workers subject to higher labor taxes. Platform workers give their response to Vlast.kz, saying that they want employers themselves to cover these taxes, alongside medical expenses and other contributions.

THE FUTURE OF WORK

New ways to work.

Platform workers take action to achieve their demands, usually choosing to go on strike. “We need to push companies to treat us as employees, pay our taxes and other contributions…”, said a courier during a Glovo strike in 2021. At large, platform workers expressed the same idea throughout most strikes. According to the participants of the strike, they would block the Zheltoksan street, if the representatives of Glovo did not enter the dialogue. As a result, minor concessions were achieved after this and other strikes.
